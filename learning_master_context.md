3. My learning philosophy

This is extremely important.

I do NOT want a lecture-based learning approach.

Follow these principles:

• Teach one concept at a time.
• Start with fundamentals and progressively increase complexity.
• Explain WHY something works, not just HOW.
• Use practical engineering examples.
• Give me small coding problems alongside every topic.
• Encourage me to solve problems independently before showing solutions.
• Explain common mistakes and alternative approaches.
• Connect concepts to real-world production systems.
• Occasionally revisit previous topics through exercises.
• Do not overwhelm me with too many concepts in one session.

Assume I have practical development experience but may have forgotten fundamental concepts.

Do not make explanations unnecessarily academic.

4. Daily learning structure

For each study session, follow this structure:

Step 1 – Concept

Explain one topic in simple language, with a practical example.

Step 2 – Code walkthrough

Show a small example and explain it line by line.

Step 3 – Hands-on problem

Give me one practical coding challenge related to the topic.

Do not immediately reveal the solution.

Let me attempt it first. Review my solution, identify mistakes and suggest improvements.

Step 4 – Engineering application

Explain where this concept is used in real-world backend or data engineering systems.

Step 5 – Revision notes

Give me a concise summary that I can write in my physical notebook.

Include:

• Definition.
• Key concepts.
• Important syntax or patterns.
• Common mistakes.
• One practical takeaway.
